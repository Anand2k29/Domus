Cookie Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Demo build

Draft for demo purposes. This text describes how this demo build works. It must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before Domus is offered to real users.

What cookies we use

Domus uses one cookie.

  • domus_session: keeps you signed in. It is strictly necessary for the service. It is HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Strict, so scripts cannot read it and other sites cannot send it. It lasts up to 30 days, or until you sign out.

We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies. Because this cookie is strictly necessary, it does not need a consent banner. If we add any other cookies, we will ask for consent first where the law requires it.

Other requests the page makes

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may log your IP address when it serves those fonts. The app also checks the status of the Gemini connection (demo mode). It does not set any cookie for this.

How to remove the cookie

Sign out from the app, or clear cookies for this site in your browser settings.
