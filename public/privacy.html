Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Demo build

Draft for demo purposes. This text describes how this demo build works. It must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before Domus is offered to real users.

1. Who we are

Domus is a flatmate-matching service. In this build, Domus is operated as a demo. The data controller contact is [controller name and address, to be added before launch].

2. What we collect

  • Sign-in details: your mobile number. We store it only as a keyed hash plus the last four digits, so the full number is not kept in readable form. Sign-in codes expire after five minutes.
  • Profile: name, age, gender, occupation, area, budget range, move-in timing, habits (sleep, cleaning, cooking, smoking, pets, guests, work mode), a deposit amount you enter, and the "about me" text you write.
  • Visibility choices: whether you appear in Discover, and whether your area and budget are shown to others.
  • Shared-home records: flatmate names, expenses and balances, agreement inputs and drafts, and requests you send.
  • Technical data: a session cookie, and per-user counters that limit how often AI features are used.

We do not store the text of scam checks. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

3. Why we use it

  • To run matching, show match scores, and send requests.
  • To keep shared expenses and agreement drafts working.
  • To keep the service secure and to prevent misuse, including the AI usage limits.

4. AI processing (Google Gemini)

Some features send text to Google's Gemini API to create summaries, match notes, scam assessments and agreement drafts. Before sending, we remove phone numbers, email addresses, UPI IDs, links and ID-style numbers. We send only the fields each task needs. We do not send your name, your phone number or your exact area to the AI.

AI output can be wrong. Scam assessments and agreement drafts are advisory and are not legal advice. Google's own terms for the API key in use also apply to the text sent.

5. Who sees your information

  • Other users see the profile fields you allow in your visibility settings. Your phone number is never shown to other users.
  • Service providers that process data for us: Cloudflare (hosting and database) and Google (Gemini API and Google Fonts). We do not sell personal data.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete your account. Sign-in codes expire after five minutes. Sessions expire after 30 days. AI usage counters are removed after the window they count toward.

7. Your rights

You can download a copy of your data and delete your account from the Profile screen in the app. You can edit your profile at any time. For other requests, contact us at the address in section 1. We will respond within the time set by applicable law.

We will honour the rights granted under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 as its rules take effect. The grievance contact will be published before launch: [grievance officer contact, to be added].

8. Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Session tokens are stored only as hashes and sent in HttpOnly cookies. Phone numbers are stored as keyed hashes. We use a content security policy and restrict access by user. No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

9. Who can use Domus

Domus is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

10. Demo limitations

In this demo, the sign-in code is shown on screen because no SMS service is connected. The ID verification step is a mock and does not check any document. The demo sample ledger and demo accounts contain sample data.

11. Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above. For material changes, we will tell you in the app.
