Under the hood

How Domus uses Gemini API keys.

Domus calls Google's Gemini API for profile summaries, match notes, scam checks and agreement drafts. Calls are tried in this order:

  1. 01key-1 · model-aTried firstThe fastest model answers, if its quota allows.
  2. 02key-1 · model-bNext modelUsed when model A fails or its quota is spent.
  3. 03key-2 · model-aNext keyThe same model order, on the second key.
  4. 04key-2 · model-bSecond modelThe backup model on the second key.
  5. 05key-3 · models a+bLast keyOnly if both earlier keys are used up.
  6. ✓built-in rulesAlways answersIf every call fails, Domus answers with its own rules and says so on screen.

Keys stay on the server. They are never sent to the browser. Free-tier limits are small, so the built-in rules keep the demo working.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Is it free?

The demo is free to try. Domus does not move rent or deposits in this build.

Is my phone number shared?

No. It is stored only as a keyed hash plus the last four digits.

Are the ID checks real?

Not in this demo. The ID step is a mock and the app says so.

Can I trust the scam check and agreement?

Treat both as a starting point. Agreement drafts are not legal advice.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Download everything as a file, then delete your account from Profile.
