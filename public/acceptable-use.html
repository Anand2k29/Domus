Acceptable Use and AI Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Demo build

Draft for demo purposes. This text describes how this demo build works. It must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before Domus is offered to real users.

What you may not do

  • Pretend to be someone else, or create a profile using another person's photos, name or details.
  • Post false listings, fake reviews, or messages meant to trick people into paying.
  • Share someone else's phone number, bank details, ID numbers or other sensitive information without their permission.
  • Harass, threaten or discriminate against anyone. Domus does not offer religion, caste or community filters, and you may not use it to search for people on those grounds.
  • Scrape, copy or resell profiles, or use automated tools against the service.
  • Try to break, overload or probe the service. This includes attempts to bypass rate limits, sign-in codes or access controls.
  • Use the AI features to produce content that is abusive, unlawful, or intended to deceive.

AI features

Domus uses Google Gemini for some summaries, match notes, scam assessments and agreement drafts. AI output can be wrong or incomplete. Check it before you act on it. You may not present AI output as professional, legal or financial advice from Domus. Usage is limited per account to keep the service fair.

Reporting problems

To report a profile, a message or a security issue, contact [contact address, to be added]. Please do not test security issues on accounts that are not yours.

Enforcement

We may remove content, limit features or close accounts that break this policy. Where we can, we will tell you why.
